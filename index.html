TO MY BII • WITH ALL MY LOVE

Dear Bii,

Happy 3 years and 7 months to us, my love. ❤️

Sometimes I look at how far we've come and I can't help but smile. Three years and seven months sounds like a long time, but when I think about all the memories we've made, it feels like there is still so much more I want to experience with you.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for being part of my life. And thank you for giving me a love story I can call ours.

Forever yours,
